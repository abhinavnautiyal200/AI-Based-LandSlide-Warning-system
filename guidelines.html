NDMA Guidelines — Landslide Management

Guidelines issued under the Disaster Management Act, 2005 for management and mitigation of landslide hazards in India.

Before a Landslide — Preparedness

  1. Check if your area is landslide-prone using GSI Susceptibility Maps.
  2. Watch for new cracks in walls, ground, or roads; tilting trees or poles; unusual water seepage.
  3. Prepare an emergency kit — documents, medicines, torch, radio, water, dry food.
  4. Know evacuation routes and safe shelter locations.
  5. Avoid construction on steep slopes or near drainage channels.
  6. Ensure proper drainage around buildings in hilly areas.
  7. Plant trees on slopes to prevent soil erosion.
  8. Register for SMS alerts from NLMS and local authorities.

During a Landslide — Immediate Action

  1. Stay alert — listen for cracking trees, boulders, or rumbling sounds.
  2. Evacuate immediately if in a landslide-prone area and you receive a warning.
  3. Move laterally to the nearest high ground. Do not try to outrun it downhill.
  4. Avoid river valleys and low-lying areas where debris may collect.
  5. If indoors — take cover under sturdy furniture. Stay away from windows.
  6. If driving — stop and move away from the road.
  7. Alert neighbours — help elderly, disabled, and children to evacuate.
  8. Call 112 or 1800-180-1111 for emergency assistance.

After a Landslide — Recovery

  1. Do not enter the slide area — additional slides may occur.
  2. Check for injured and trapped persons. Report to rescue teams.
  3. Watch for flooding from debris dams.
  4. Check building foundation and walls before re-entry.
  5. Report broken utility lines (electricity, water, gas) to authorities.
  6. Follow instructions from local District Disaster Management Authority.
  7. Seek professional assessment before rebuilding.
