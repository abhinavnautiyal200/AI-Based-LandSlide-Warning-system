Do's & Don'ts

✅ Do's

  • Move to higher ground immediately
  • Listen for unusual sounds like rumbling
  • Keep emergency kit ready during monsoon
  • Report any cracks to local authorities
  • Follow evacuation orders without delay

❌ Don'ts

  • Don't build houses near steep slopes
  • Don't ignore early warning signs
  • Don't cut trees on hill slopes
  • Don't block natural drainage channels
  • Don't panic — stay calm and alert

About NLMS

The National Landslide Monitoring System (NLMS) is an initiative under the Ministry of Earth Sciences, Government of India. It provides real-time monitoring, early warning, and risk assessment for landslide-prone regions across the country. Data is collected from GSI, IMD, NRSC, and various state-level disaster management authorities.

Around 12.6% of India's land area is susceptible to landslides. The NLMS currently monitors over 147 high-risk zones using a combination of ground-based sensors, satellite images, rainfall data, and predictive modelling. Alerts are color-coded as RED ORANGE YELLOW GREEN and sent out via SMS, email, and this web portal.
